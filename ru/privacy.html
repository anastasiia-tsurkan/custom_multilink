Privacy Policy

International Ministry of Charity

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

International Ministry of Charity ("IMOCE," "we," "our," or "us") is a nonprofit organization registered in Washington State, USA, and recognized as a 501(c)(3) public charity.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, donation forms, and communications.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when voluntarily provided.
  • Donation details, including amounts, dates, and donation frequency.
  • Information necessary to process donations through our payment providers.
  • Messages or inquiries you send to us.
  • Technical information, such as browser type, device information, and website usage data, where collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Process donations and provide donation confirmations or receipts.
  • Manage recurring donations and donor relationships.
  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Share updates about our humanitarian projects and mission activities.
  • Maintain and improve our website and donation services.
  • Meet applicable legal, financial, and reporting obligations.

We do not sell donor personal information.

3. Donation Processing

Online donations may be processed through third-party services, including GivingFuel, Webconnex Payments, PayPal, Venmo, and Cash App, depending on the donation method selected.

These providers process payment information according to their own security practices and privacy policies.

IMOCE does not require donors to send payment card information directly by email.

4. Information Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our website, process donations, maintain records, or communicate with supporters.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information. Payment transactions are handled through third-party payment providers.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including financial recordkeeping, legal compliance, and donor communications.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

You may also request to stop receiving optional communications from IMOCE.

8. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website and third-party donation services may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for functionality, security, or website analytics.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate information and choices regarding nonessential tracking technologies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children under 13.

We do not knowingly solicit such information through our website.

10. International Operations

IMOCE supports charitable projects in multiple countries. Information may be processed in the United States or other locations where our authorized service providers operate, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact:

International Ministry of Charity

Washington State, USA

Email: imocemmanuel@gmail.com

Website: https://imoce.org
