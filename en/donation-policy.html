Donation & Refund Policy

International Ministry of Charity

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

Thank you for supporting International Ministry of Charity (IMOCE). Your generosity helps us provide education, meals, humanitarian assistance, and other essential support to communities in need.

1. Charitable Donations

International Ministry of Charity is recognized as a 501(c)(3) public charity in the United States.

Donations are generally tax-deductible for eligible U.S. taxpayers to the extent permitted by law.

Tax treatment may vary based on individual circumstances and applicable regulations. Donors should consult a qualified tax professional when necessary.

2. How Donations Are Used

Donations support IMOCE's charitable activities, including education, food assistance, humanitarian programs, and related operational needs.

Donations designated for a particular campaign or program are intended to support that purpose.

If circumstances change or a project receives funding beyond its needs, the use of remaining restricted funds will be determined according to applicable law and the terms of the donation.

3. One-Time Donations

A one-time donation is processed for the amount authorized by the donor at checkout.

Once successfully processed, the donor will receive a confirmation or receipt through the applicable payment service, where supported.

4. Recurring Donations

Donors may choose to make recurring contributions.

By selecting a recurring donation, you authorize the selected payment provider to charge your chosen payment method according to the amount and frequency displayed during checkout.

Recurring donations continue until canceled or otherwise ended under the applicable payment arrangement.

5. Canceling Recurring Donations

You may request cancellation of your recurring donation at any time by contacting:

imocemmanuel@gmail.com

Please include your name and the email address associated with your donation.

For donations processed through GivingFuel, donors may also be able to manage or cancel recurring donations through the GivingFuel Donor Account Center.

Cancellation stops future scheduled payments once processed and does not automatically refund previous donations.

6. Refund Requests

Because donations are charitable contributions, they are generally considered final.

However, we understand that payment errors may occur.

If you believe a donation was made in error, duplicated, or processed without your authorization, please contact us at:

imocemmanuel@gmail.com

Include the donation date, amount, payment method, and a brief explanation of the issue. Do not send full payment card numbers or banking credentials.

Refund requests will be reviewed individually, subject to applicable law, payment provider requirements, and transaction status.

Approved refunds will generally be issued through the original payment method where supported. Processing times may vary.

7. Payment Processing

Online donations may be processed through GivingFuel, Webconnex Payments, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, or other payment methods displayed on our official donation pages.

Payment providers may apply transaction fees or processing requirements.

Any optional donor-paid fees should be disclosed during the donation process.

8. Donation Receipts

Donation confirmations or receipts may be issued electronically.

For questions about receipts or donation records, please contact imocemmanuel@gmail.com.

9. Contact Information

For questions about donations, recurring contributions, cancellations, or refunds:

International Ministry of Charity

Washington State, USA

Email: imocemmanuel@gmail.com

Website: https://imoce.org
